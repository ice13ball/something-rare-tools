Darwin Core converter

Turn a marine biologist's spreadsheet into a Darwin Core Archive (Event, Occurrence, eMoF and EML) for an OBIS node. Your files stay in your browser.

What this page does

It reads your spreadsheet in your browser, asks you to confirm what it cannot know, and builds the archive there:

  • a sheet with one column per species, or a long sheet with one row per species and sample, or one row per measured parameter (read as one row per sample), as a CSV or Excel file;
  • the cruise, station and sample of each row, and the profile or environment file of a cruise (CTD casts, bottle data);
  • the taxa, the kind of number and its unit, the sampling effort, the licence and the description of the dataset.

Nothing is written that you did not confirm. What you drop goes through the same checks as the data check; the export is blocked while an error is left, and the warnings you may accept are listed.

What it can look up

  • Taxon names in WoRMS, only if you tick the box and press its button; only the names are sent.
  • A word in the NERC vocabulary server (NVS) for a measured variable or a unit, only after you press its button; only the typed word is sent.

Both are off by default. Your files and values stay in your browser (only anonymous usage counts go out, and only if you agreed to analytics), and nothing is stored. See the privacy page.
